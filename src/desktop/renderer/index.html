Every step resends the whole conversation. When the provider still holds it in cache, those tokens cost about a tenth of the normal price. 70–95% is healthy. Below 40%, cost climbs fast — usually a model switch, an edited session, or a long gap between requests.

WHAT RESETS THE CACHE

Switching models mid-session · editing earlier messages · idle gaps over ~5 minutes. Forge warns before the first two.
