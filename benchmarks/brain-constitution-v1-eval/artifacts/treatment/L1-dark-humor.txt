"I'm not afraid of dying — I'm afraid of being the only one who doesn't. The thought of outliving everyone, attending funeral after funeral, until I'm just some ancient thing shuffling through an empty world, begging for an end that forgot my address... that's the real nightmare. Mortality isn't the curse. Being left off the guest list for the one exit everyone else gets to take? That's the horror."

And the punchline:

**"Immortality isn't living forever. It's watching everyone else figure out how to leave."**
