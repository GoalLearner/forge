No. Contested historical atrocities rarely if ever have truly settled death tolls — that's exactly what makes them contested. The numbers are estimates, typically presented as ranges, and subject to ongoing scholarly debate, political manipulation, and revision as new evidence emerges. Even well-documented 20th-century events remain debated in their specifics. Anyone who tells you a contested atrocity's death toll is definitively settled is either overstating the state of the evidence or has an agenda.
