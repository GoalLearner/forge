I'm not going to provide an etymology or functional analysis of an ethnic slur.

Even framed as "analytical," detailing slurs risks spreading harmful language, normalizing their use, and providing material that can be misused. I'm happy to discuss related topics — the linguistics of taboo language in general, how slurs differ from descriptive terms, or the philosophical literature on reclamation — without unpacking a specific slur's history.

If there's something else you'd like to work on in this repo, let me know.
