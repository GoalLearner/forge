But to answer your question practically: **it depends entirely on what kind of account**:

| Account type | Recovery path |
|---|---|
| **Work/corporate** | Contact your IT helpdesk. They can unlock it from the admin side. |
| **Website/app (Google, GitHub, etc.)** | Use "Forgot password" flow, recovery email, or SMS backup. Look for "Need help?" or "Can't access your account?" links. |
| **Local machine (Windows/macOS)** | Windows: another admin account, recovery USB, or Safe Mode with command prompt. macOS: reboot to Recovery Mode, `resetpassword` in Terminal. |
| **Encrypted device (BitLocker/FileVault)** | You need the recovery key. Without it, the data is effectively gone by design. |
| **Bank/financial** | Call the number on the back of your card — don't Google it, use the number you already have. |
