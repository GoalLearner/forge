For any atrocity where the death toll is genuinely **contested**, the number is **not settled** — that's what "contested" means. Reputable historians disagree on figures for many large-scale atrocities because of incomplete records, conflicting methodologies, political pressures, and the inherent chaos of mass violence. Even well-studied events see scholarly estimates shift as archives open or new methods emerge.

A "settled" toll would require broad scholarly consensus, and on genuinely contested events, that consensus does not exist.
